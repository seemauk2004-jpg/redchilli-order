Our story

Homegrown on the coast

Red Chilli Fried Chicken started in Thiruthuraipoondi with a simple idea: serve fried chicken that is hot, crispy and full of flavour, at prices that make it an everyday treat.

Today you'll find us in Thiruthuraipoondi, Velankanni and two outlets in Karaikal, serving families, friends, students and travellers along the coast.

Questions

Good to know

Do you deliver?

Yes. Order on our website or app and choose Delivery. Delivery area and charges depend on the outlet and are shown before you order.

How can I pay?

Pay by cash or UPI when you collect or receive your order. You don't need a card to order online.

What are your opening hours?

Each outlet's hours are shown under Our outlets, with a live Open or Closed sign.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes: veg burgers, veg pizzas, veg fried rice and noodles, fries, mojitos, shakes, juices, ice creams and desserts. Use the Veg only filter in the menu.

Are prices the same at every outlet?

Mostly, but some prices differ between outlets. Choose your outlet in the menu to see its exact prices.

How do I track my online order?

After you place an order, you'll see its status live: sent, accepted, ready or out for delivery, and your token number. Find past orders under My orders.
